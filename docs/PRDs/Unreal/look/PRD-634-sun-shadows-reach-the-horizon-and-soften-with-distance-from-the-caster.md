# PRD-634 — Sun shadows reach the horizon and soften with distance from the caster

**Status:** PARTIAL
**Priority:** P2 — template integration and native parity remain open: the starter's sun shadow ends 14 m from the player and has the same edge width everywhere, while Unreal's default shadows cover the view and widen with the gap between caster and receiver.
**Complexity:** 6 (MEDIUM) — a shadow-filter mechanism in core (+2), the shadow method choice across 13 templates (+2), native proof of both (+2); risk override: the templates already reverted a virtual-shadow default once (level-boundary artefacts), so Phase 1 measures before anything changes.
**Owner:** João
**Depends on:** [PRD-631](PRD-631-an-unreal-reference-scene-measures-the-default-look.md) Phase 3 (the L5 row). Coordinates with [PRD-457](../rendering/PRD-457-virtual-shadows-scale-by-measurement.md), which owns virtual-shadow page cost. Builds on [PRD-572](../../done/PRD-572-shadow-casters-sort-themselves-into-static-and-moving.md) and [PRD-538](../../done/PRD-538-shadow-maps-redraw-only-what-streamed-in.md).

## Context

**ThreeNative today:**

- The starter draws one `PCFSoftShadowMap` sun map: 4096² on desktop, 2048² on phones, with an
  orthographic half-extent of 14 m and near/far 1/80 m
  (`packages/create-threenative/templates/starter/src/render/lighting.ts:11-26`). Other templates
  widen the box to 22–42 m. Past that box, nothing casts a shadow.
- The authored `radius: 2` changes nothing on the native engine: its PCF-soft branch is a fixed 3×3
  footprint (`packages/runtime-native/src/engine/shader/standard.cpp:719`). So the edge width is
  one constant, whatever the gap between caster and receiver.
- Core ships `VirtualShadowNode`, camera-following clip levels with a page cache
  (`packages/core/src/render/virtual-shadow.ts:867`). The templates stopped using it after level
  boundaries showed as shadows that suddenly turned sharp (`minimal/src/render/lighting.ts`
  comment). The done PRD-470 still claims `minimal` uses it, which is stale.
- [PRD-561](../../done/PRD-561-contact-shadows-from-screen-depth.md) declined screen-space contact shadows on 2026-10-10. Unreal agrees by default: its light's contact-shadow length is 0.

**Unreal 5.8.3** (source read 2026-10-19):

| Setting | Default | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Method | Virtual shadow maps, turned on by the project generator | `Engine/Source/Editor/GameProjectGeneration/Private/GameProjectUtils.cpp:136` |
| Clipmap | 128² pages, 16384² virtual resolution, directional levels 6 to 22 | `Engine/Shaders/Shared/VirtualShadowMapDefinitions.h:13-20`; `Engine/Source/Runtime/Renderer/Private/VirtualShadowMaps/VirtualShadowMapClipmap.cpp:57-63` |
| Penumbra | Shadow-map ray tracing (SMRT): 8 rays × 4 samples at High and Epic, from the light's source angle | `Engine/Config/BaseScalability.ini:224,262` |
| Sun source angle | 0.5357° | `Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/Components/DirectionalLightComponent.cpp:1050` |
| Contact shadows | Off (length 0) | `Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/Components/LightComponent.cpp:470` |
| Fallback without VSM | 4 cascades to 400 m, distribution exponent 3 | `DirectionalLightComponent.cpp:1038-1055` |

At 0.5357°, the penumbra is about 0.94 cm wide for each metre between the caster and the receiver.
A capsule's feet stay sharp, and a shadow cast from 10 m up spreads to about 9 cm.

## Solution

1. **Phase 1 decides the method by measurement, on the L5 cells.** It compares three candidates
   against the Unreal frames and the `high` frame budget:
   - the current single map widened to the view
   - three.js cascades
   - `VirtualShadowNode` with the boundary artefact fixed

   The winner becomes the template default. The decision and its numbers go under `## Decisions`.
   Phase 1 also states the artefact's cause before it rules on virtual shadows.
2. **A source-angle soft filter in core.** It searches the shadow map for the average blocker depth
   and widens the filter by `gap × tan(sourceAngle)`, clamped to the map's texel size. The angle
   comes from the game, and the templates set Unreal's 0.5357°. The filter is a mechanism:
   - it does not decide the look, because the angle is a game value
   - it runs on any of the three methods

   It replaces the fixed 3×3 on the native engine as well.
3. **Contact shadows stay off**, as in Unreal (PRD-561 holds).

## Decisions

- 2026-10-21 (the sphere-edge round ends NEUTRAL; harness defaults stay as they are): the arm with Unreal's penumbra scale, six square levels, a LOD rung per level and no pixel floor (`a-f0`) was judged against the epic arm (`vsmLodTexels=Infinity`) at L9, twice per arm, on full frames and on 2x crops of the sphere row. Both verdicts are NEUTRAL with no REGRESSION (sun 4 leak and smear are gone, which `84bc6d33c` broke). A floor of 0.5 pixels (`a-f05`) over-blurs and 0.3 (`a-f03`, L5 only) did not beat 0, which is closest to Unreal, so `minPenumbraPixels` stays opt-in and the default of the engine is unchanged. I did not flip the harness defaults (`vsmAngle=ue`, `vsmExt=16,8,4,2,1,0.5`, `vsmMover=128`), since a NEUTRAL round does not earn a change that moves every lane's baseline. Next lever for a successor: shadow edges need texels near one pixel at the sphere row, not the 6.8 mm texel of a 7 m level. Try a per-level resolution (`mapSize` per level, or page-level LOD bias on the sun) and a temporal accumulation of the filter taps, which is what makes Unreal's edge clean, before any more sun-disc tuning. AC-3 (frame-budget meter, sun 60 exterior at L9, exclusive GPU window, BEFORE/AFTER alternated twice, `frame` mean/p95 in ms of the last window): BEFORE 6.0/14.8 and 4.4/9.3, AFTER 4.2/9.2 and 3.8/7.5, so six levels cost nothing measurable on the RTX 2080; the proof named in AC-3 (`playtest perf`) did not run, so the box stays open.
- 2026-10-21 (a level draws the coarsest LOD rung within one texel, not the coarsest on every level): the harness sphere and capsule shadows had straight facets with six clip levels. Cause: `shadowLodBias` drew the coarsest AutoLOD rung of every caster on every clip level past the finest (`virtual-shadow.ts` `#probe`; native `renderer.cpp`, the virtual page draw). A sphere shadow that falls in level 2 (6.8 mm texels) came from a 98 triangle rung with 60 mm of error. Ruled out first: the source meshes are smooth (spheres 48 by 24, capsule 32 by 16 in `tools/unreal-reference/build_scene.py`). Contour roughness (RMS residual of the 50 percent edge from its own degree-4 fit, mean over the lower edges of four ground shadows of the L5 sun 60 `spheres` pose, `~/.cache/tmp-tn/horizon/rough5.py`): Unreal 0.16 px, epic default 0.28 px, six levels with the coarsest rung 0.64 px (worst edge 1.0 px). Fix: a level takes the coarsest rung whose error, at the mesh's world scale and the batch's instance scale, is within `shadowLodTexels` texels of that level (default 1), and never a rung finer than the main pass shows. `shadowLodTexels: Infinity` is the old rule and `shadowLodBias: false` still draws the main pass geometry. Native has the same rule (`shadowLodRung` in `atlas.h`, called from `renderer.cpp` per page; the `lod` command carries the rungs and their errors). The GPU scene's keyed path (`base: COARSEST_SHADOW_LEVEL`) is not changed.
- 2026-10-21 (the pixel floor is not Unreal's `TexelDitherScaleDirectional`): `sourceAngleShadowFilter({ minPenumbraPixels })` adds the receiver's own screen pixel, per axis in each level's uv, to the geometric penumbra in quadrature, capped at 16 texels (default 0, so no game changes; native `virtual_sun_pixel_floor` measures the 10 to 90 percent edge width of a plate shadow at 0.86 px, 2.12 px and 3.41 px for floors 0, 1 and 2). It stands in for the temporal resolve that gives Unreal its 2 px sun edges: `layers.json` keeps TSR on in every lit layer, and TN had no temporal AA until PRD-636. Unreal's `r.Shadow.Virtual.SMRT.TexelDitherScaleDirectional` (2.0) is another thing: it jitters each ray start by about half a texel of the selected level (`VirtualShadowMapProjectionDirectional.ush:214-219`, `:246`), which the owned engine's half-texel disc floor already does. No rename, and re-tune the floor now that TRAA is the `high` default.
- 2026-10-21 (Unreal's sun disc is not normalised, so its penumbra is not a constant 0.94 cm per metre): `GetRandomDirectionalLightRayDir` (`VirtualShadowMapProjectionDirectional.ush:153-162`) builds the disc axes as `cross(L, x)` and `cross(that, L)` (L the unit light direction, x the world x axis) and never normalises them, so the disc radius is `sin(angle / 2)` (`DirectionalLightComponent.cpp:402`) times the square root of `1 - L.x * L.x`. The `Context` figure of 0.94 cm per metre holds only for L perpendicular to x. The lab suns sit at azimuth 90, where the factor is sin(elevation): 0.87, 0.26 and 0.07 at 60, 15 and 4 degrees. The starter's L5 suns sit at azimuth 25: 0.98, 0.91 and 0.91. The harness knob `vsmAngle=ue` applies this factor (`unrealSourceScale` in `Reference.ts.in`); the engine keeps the game-owned `sourceAngleRad`. Lab result (penumbra-lab, TN edge width over Unreal's, order sun 60, 15, 4 by contact, close, remote, pass band 0.75 to 1.25): the epic default (full angle, no floor, four square levels) is 0.64 1.09 1.45 1.43 3.08 2.92 2.31 3.93 5.18, 1 cell in 9 in band. Unreal's scale with a floor of 0.4 px and six square levels (`16,8,4,2,1,0.5` divisors of the reach, mover maps 128) is 0.79 1.12 1.11 0.86 1.21 1.14 0.80 1.04 0.84, 9 of 9. A floor of 0.5 gives 8 of 9 and 0.6 gives 7 of 9. Seven levels with 0.5 give 9 of 9 at a larger texture, so six stay.
- 2026-10-21 (windows fitted to the ground are reverted, square windows stay the default): `84bc6d33c` fitted each level's light-space height to the ground (`minWindowAspect` 0.25). A fresh judge saw a lit patch break the dark band of the sun 4 ramp and no edge gain at sun 4: verdict REGRESSION (sun 4 leak and smear), a failed round. A level centred on the eye holds the floor only about `eye height / tan(elevation)` from the camera, and a wide filter disc crossing the level's top edge is the likely cause of the patch (not proven). The default is 1 (square) again; `minWindowAspect` stays the named opt-in, and its JSDoc lists the costs.

- 2026-10-19: Contact shadows stay declined (PRD-561). Unreal's default light has contact-shadow
  length 0, so the reference does not ask for them.
- 2026-10-19: Unreal's SMRT traces rays through the shadow map. This PRD copies the effect (a
