# PRD-634 — Sun shadows reach the horizon and soften with distance from the caster

**Status:** PARTIAL
**Priority:** P2 — template integration and native parity remain open: the starter's sun shadow ends 14 m from the player and has the same edge width everywhere, while Unreal's default shadows cover the view and widen with the gap between caster and receiver.
**Complexity:** 6 (MEDIUM) — a shadow-filter mechanism in core (+2), the shadow method choice across 13 templates (+2), native proof of both (+2); risk override: the templates already reverted a virtual-shadow default once (level-boundary artefacts), so Phase 1 measures before anything changes.
**Owner:** João
**Depends on:** [PRD-631](../../done/PRD-631-an-unreal-reference-scene-measures-the-default-look.md) Phase 3 (the L5 row). Coordinates with [PRD-457](../rendering/PRD-457-virtual-shadows-scale-by-measurement.md), which owns virtual-shadow page cost. Builds on [PRD-572](../../done/PRD-572-shadow-casters-sort-themselves-into-static-and-moving.md) and [PRD-538](../../done/PRD-538-shadow-maps-redraw-only-what-streamed-in.md).

## Context

**ThreeNative today:**

- The starter draws one `PCFSoftShadowMap` sun map: 4096² on desktop, 2048² on phones, with an
  orthographic half-extent of 14 m and near/far 1/80 m
  (`packages/create-threenative/templates/starter/src/render/lighting.ts:11-26`). Other templates
  widen the box to 22–42 m. Past that box, nothing casts a shadow.
- The authored `radius: 2` changes nothing on the native engine: its PCF-soft branch is a fixed 3×3
  footprint (`packages/runtime-native/src/engine/shader/standard.cpp:719`). So the edge width is
  one constant, whatever the gap between caster and receiver.
- Core ships `VirtualShadowNode`, camera-following clip levels with a page cache
  (`packages/core/src/render/virtual-shadow.ts:867`). The templates stopped using it after level
  boundaries showed as shadows that suddenly turned sharp (`minimal/src/render/lighting.ts`
  comment). The done PRD-470 still claims `minimal` uses it, which is stale.
- [PRD-561](../../done/PRD-561-contact-shadows-from-screen-depth.md) declined screen-space contact shadows on 2026-10-10. Unreal agrees by default: its light's contact-shadow length is 0.

**Unreal 5.8.3** (source read 2026-10-19):

| Setting | Default | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Method | Virtual shadow maps, turned on by the project generator | `Engine/Source/Editor/GameProjectGeneration/Private/GameProjectUtils.cpp:136` |
| Clipmap | 128² pages, 16384² virtual resolution, directional levels 6 to 22 | `Engine/Shaders/Shared/VirtualShadowMapDefinitions.h:13-20`; `Engine/Source/Runtime/Renderer/Private/VirtualShadowMaps/VirtualShadowMapClipmap.cpp:57-63` |
| Penumbra | Shadow-map ray tracing (SMRT): 8 rays × 4 samples at High and Epic, from the light's source angle | `Engine/Config/BaseScalability.ini:224,262` |
| Sun source angle | 0.5357° | `Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/Components/DirectionalLightComponent.cpp:1050` |
| Contact shadows | Off (length 0) | `Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/Components/LightComponent.cpp:470` |
| Fallback without VSM | 4 cascades to 400 m, distribution exponent 3 | `DirectionalLightComponent.cpp:1038-1055` |

At 0.5357°, the penumbra is about 0.94 cm wide for each metre between the caster and the receiver.
A capsule's feet stay sharp, and a shadow cast from 10 m up spreads to about 9 cm.

## Solution

1. **Phase 1 decides the method by measurement, on the L5 cells.** It compares three candidates
   against the Unreal frames and the `high` frame budget:
   - the current single map widened to the view
   - three.js cascades
   - `VirtualShadowNode` with the boundary artefact fixed

   The winner becomes the template default. The decision and its numbers go under `## Decisions`.
   Phase 1 also states the artefact's cause before it rules on virtual shadows.
2. **A source-angle soft filter in core.** It searches the shadow map for the average blocker depth
   and widens the filter by `gap × tan(sourceAngle)`, clamped to the map's texel size. The angle
   comes from the game, and the templates set Unreal's 0.5357°. The filter is a mechanism:
   - it does not decide the look, because the angle is a game value
   - it runs on any of the three methods

   It replaces the fixed 3×3 on the native engine as well.
3. **Contact shadows stay off**, as in Unreal (PRD-561 holds).

- 2026-10-22 (each clip level takes its map size from Unreal's texel rule; the 3072 number is gone; vsm2-lane, code `44b37910e`, merged with the epic at `a71b05022`): Unreal marks a receiver's page in level `floor(log2(distance) + ResolutionLodBiasDirectional + log2(LodScale))`, `LodScale = 0.5 / ProjectionScale.X * 16384 / ViewportWidth` (`Engine/Source/Runtime/Renderer/Private/VirtualShadowMaps/VirtualShadowMapClipmap.cpp:245-254`, `Engine/Shaders/Private/VirtualShadowMaps/VirtualShadowMapPageMarking.ush:35-39`). A level's radius is `2^(level+1)` (`VirtualShadowMapClipmap.cpp:175-179`) and its ortho half size is twice that (`:363`, `:452`), so one texel covers 2^bias to 2^(1+bias) of the receiver's screen pixels. The entry below says "at most one pixel at bias 0"; that is 2x too fine. **The rule, in both engines:** each level's map size makes its texel at most 2^(1+bias) screen pixels at the nearest receiver that the level serves (inner distance 0.775 times the next finer level's extent, the selection guard minus the refresh step; the finest level uses the same ratio), rounded up to 128 and clamped to 128..4096. When the pages pass `maxPhysicalPages` (default 4096, Unreal's Epic `r.Shadow.Virtual.MaxPhysicalPages`, `Engine/Config/BaseScalability.ini:257`) the bias rises in steps of 0.25 until they fit (Unreal's pool rule, `Engine/Source/Runtime/Renderer/Private/VirtualShadowMaps/VirtualShadowMapCacheManager.cpp:703-717`); native also raises it until the packed atlas fits the device's `maxTextureDimension2D`. **Auto by default, measured where used:** web core reads the camera's field of view and the drawing buffer once at setup (a resize re-versions textures); native reads `1 / P[0]` and the renderer width on every main-view frame and rebuilds the atlas when the sizes change (reflection passes measure nothing). **Named override:** `mapSize` (one number, or one per level) on `VirtualShadowNode` and on the native binding, `maxPhysicalPages` on both, `autoMapSize` and `resolutionLodBiasDirectional` on the binding; harness `vsmMap=N`. The native page table is per level (a level's base is the sum of tiles squared of the finer levels, header row `k*9+6`). Contour roughness (RMS residual of the 50 percent edge from its own degree-4 fit, mean over the lower edges of four ground shadows of the L5 sun 60 `spheres` pose, `~/.cache/tmp-tn/horizon/rough5.py`): Unreal 0.16 px, epic default 0.28 px, six levels with the coarsest rung 0.64 px (worst edge 1.0 px). - 2026-10-21 (the pixel floor is not Unreal's `TexelDitherScaleDirectional`): `sourceAngleShadowFilter({ minPenumbraPixels })` adds the receiver's own screen pixel, per axis in each level's uv, to the geometric penumbra in quadrature, capped at 16 texels (default 0, so no game changes; native `virtual_sun_pixel_floor` measures the 10 to 90 percent edge width of a plate shadow at 0.86 px, 2.12 px and 3.41 px for floors 0, 1 and 2). - 2026-10-21 (Unreal's sun disc is not normalised, so its penumbra is not a constant 0.94 cm per metre): `GetRandomDirectionalLightRayDir` (`VirtualShadowMapProjectionDirectional.ush:153-162`) builds the disc axes as `cross(L, x)` and `cross(that, L)` (L the unit light direction, x the world x axis) and never normalises them, so the disc radius is `sin(angle / 2)` (`DirectionalLightComponent.cpp:402`) times the square root of `1 - L.x * L.x`.
