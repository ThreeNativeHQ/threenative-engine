# Kit notes: collectathon

Kit-specific facts moved out of the kit `AGENTS.md`. Shared conventions: `conventions.md`.

## Overview

Pip, a stout sprout-keeper in a teal knit beanie and a belted mustard tunic, roams one free island for three Sunseeds: reach the summit, collect the eight red gems, climb the wall-kick shaft. The moveset is a 3D platformer's: run, single, double and triple jump, crouch, back flip, side flip, long jump, wall kick, ledge grab, dive and ground pound, with an eight-wedge health meter, coins that heal, and three kinds of walker. Every model and animation is procedural — no mesh or clip file. The tiling surfaces (grass, sand, rock, stone, bark, planks) are generated WebP images in `assets/` (`tex-*.webp`, shared by the kits and copied when a source names the file), each traced in `tex-PROVENANCE.md`; keep `assets/` at most 1.5 MB (the scaffold spec checks both), load textures in `Scene.load()` through `loadSurfaces(ctx.assets)` in `src/render/surface.ts`, and never sample a tile plainly: `stochastic`, `macro` and `triplanar` there hide its repeat. Paint a one-off texture in code with `paintedTexture` in `src/render/materials.ts`.


## Ownership

ThreeNative owns bootstrap, renderer, fixed-step loop, input, loading, physics bindings, the state bridge, and the look's render chain (`WorldEnvironment` from `@threenative/core`: the filmic tone curve and metered auto exposure). This repository owns the course, the hero's feel, the walkers, the pickups, the HUD, the camera, and the scene's own sky, sun and materials; `src/game.ts` is portable and React mounts from `src/main.ts`.


## Start every change

`@threenative/physics/navigation` is browser-only WASM, and this course needs no navigation: walkers chase in a straight line inside a leash, and the course is authored geometry, so the hero collides with exactly the triangles it sees and desktop/native stays honest.


## Commands and map

`src/entities/Hero.ts` owns the moveset: one action state machine on a `CharacterBody3D` and `HERO_FEEL`, the one table of metres and seconds (run cap 9.6 m/s, jumps 12.6 / 15.6 / 20.7 m/s up, 0.3 s chain window with a 0.12 s press buffer, one air double jump per flight at 12.6 m/s from a second press 0.1 s after take-off, 0.167 s wall-kick window that a press up to 0.12 s before the bonk also reaches (a press at a wall is a kick, never the air double), long jump ×1.5 at half gravity, ledge band 1.0–1.6 m above the feet, found by wall rays 0.3 m and 1.5 m above them) the course is laid out from — change a number there and a gap in `src/level/Course.ts` changes its meaning. The stick is camera-relative. The wall and the ledge are found with `ctx.physics.directSpaceState.intersectRay` against the static world's layer (`WORLD_LAYER`), because `moveAndSlide` reports the floor and not the wall. `Walker.ts` is the three enemy kinds, each with a telegraph before it attacks: a bumblet chaser, a hopper that crouches then hops, and a thornback that rears then charges (a stomp, a dive or a ground pound defeats a bumblet or a hopper; a thornback's spikes hurt a stomp, so only a dive or a pound beats it; a touch costs a wedge), `Pickup.ts` a coin, red gem or Sunseed; `src/scenes/Play.ts` registers every placeable entity in `load()` so a scenario's `setup.place` reaches it, then owns wedges, Sunseeds, checkpoints and the kill plane; `src/render/` is ordinary Three.js and every model in the game, `src/level/terrain.ts` shapes the island (hills, cliffs, the castle plateau) on core's `Heightfield` and keeps every route and level zone at y = 0, so place things with `course.ground(x, z)`, never a fixed height; `src/render/ground.ts` writes the blend weights `surface.ts` paints it with (grass, path sand, rock by slope), `src/render/castle.ts` is the castle and the signpost, `src/render/water.ts` the sea (tinted by a baked depth map of the island, its swell core's `WaveField` from `Course.ts`), `src/render/foliage.ts` the instanced planting, meadow and pebbles (never solid; `course.meadow.update(camera.position)` thins far grass each frame, `GRASS_SHARE` in `quality.ts` sets the share per tier), and `playtests/survives.playtest.json` asserts the relief, slope shares, castle, grass, pebbles and signpost; `src/render/effects.ts` is every particle look (the dust, flash, pollen and sparkle emitters and the `BURSTS` table), and `Play.ts` fires them through four pooled `GPUParticles3D` spawn emitters, proven by `playtests/effects.playtest.json`; `src/ui/Hud.tsx` is the one HUD. The orbit camera (`src/render/camera.ts`, turned by the mouse once a click takes the pointer lock, proven by `playtests/look.playtest.json`) and the sun are written in `afterPhysics`, never in the scene's frame function, because `moveAndSlide` only queues motion; the camera pulls in front of any wall on a ray the scene passes it, and when a wall boxes it in it cuts to the clearest side, never steeper than 35°. The orbiting platform is a kinematic `RigidBody3D` the scene moves by its mesh: the engine carries a character standing on it, so there is no carry code here. Keep `playtests/survives.playtest.json` as smoke proof and `touch-controls.ts`/`touch-layout.ts` aligned with the native playtest; `playtests/` holds one scenario per move (`jump-chain`, `double-tap`, `double-tap-air`, `acrobatics`, `wall-kick`, `ledge`, `ledge-angle`, `ledge-too-high`) and per rule (`collect`, `combat`, `enemies`, `thornback`, `telegraphs`, `camera`). Collision is the engine's, from your geometry: `buildStaticColliders(ctx, course.group, { predicate: (object) => object.userData.solid === true, reachableCeiling: 40 })` turns the tagged walkable meshes into fixed trimesh bodies; the raised ceiling keeps the 16 m shaft walls solid. A `CollisionShape3D` capsule is centred on the body's origin, so the origin is the hero's waist and its rig is pushed down by `halfHeight + radius`; forget that and the hero floats a body-length above every surface.

Play gates: `playtests/explore.playtest.json` drives 7200 ticks of seeded random input and fails on any page error; `look.playtest.json` fails when mouse motion does not turn `camera.main`. A procedural rig bounds its feet with `footSlip` on sole nodes named `footL`/`footR`: `maxSlideSpeed: 1` fails a planted sole that slides faster than 1 m/s, and unnamed soles fail closed. A new key binding joins `explore`'s `move` or `actions` in the same change.


## Portable authoring contracts

Leave `assets` absent: this kit ships no authored model, texture or sound, so the cook has nothing to do. Before adding one, read `node_modules/create-threenative/agent-docs/references/finding-assets.md`; the cook's passes, `assets.exclude` globs and `assets.budget` ceilings count bytes left uncooked and are reported either way.

No model here is imported, so there is no authored scale to normalise and the collider is the body: the generated scale-and-snap conventions do not apply to this template.

`src/render/sky.ts` builds the sky (gradient, sun halo, seeded cumulus) from `palette.ts` and uses it as both background and environment light, and `SUN_DIRECTION` aims `lighting.ts`'s one authored sun. No material backlighting ships: as in a stock Unreal scene, the sun, the sky light and each material's own colour are the whole light. The look's chain is core's, not vendored: `src/render/postprocessing.ts` calls `new WorldEnvironment(qualityPreset(tier))`, and `qualityPreset` in `src/render/quality.ts` is the `@threenative/core` preset plus this game's overrides, each with its reason — it starts empty, so the game gets the stock Unreal 5.8.3 look (`TN_WORLD_ENVIRONMENT` reports the filmic curve and `TN_AUTO_EXPOSURE` the meter), metered at 0 EV by the starter kits' shared `src/render/kitExposure.ts`. Pin `tonemapMode: "aces"`, or `autoExposureEnabled: false` with a fixed `exposure`, there and only there, and say why.
